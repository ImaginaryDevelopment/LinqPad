## Account-wide gearing (NO DROP transferable)

On this server, PoG NO DROP can go to **any char on the account**. Optimize for **empty slots filled across the whole roster**, not “who is in the group tonight.”

### Rules of thumb

1. **Farm with the strongest 40+ group** that can clear the camp safely. Puller/tank class does not need to match the loot table.
2. **Every set piece has a home.** Dupes and “wrong class for tonight” still bank to the alt who needs it.
3. **Empty slot > upgrade** until a char has a full wearable skeleton (head/arms/wrists/hands/legs/feet/chest). Then chase BP upgrades (Berserker’s over Stonewood, Melodic BP, Spirit Weaver BP, etc.).
4. **Under-40s count.** They cannot farm PoG, but they wear the same sets — shipping them gear levels them faster and frees the 40+ chars to chase rarer pieces.
5. **Jenaviev (DRU) is mostly out of PoG armor.** Keep taking Oakleaf / Thirneg / Maple Leaf utility; for a real plane armor path use **Plane of Hate** (Druid/Paladin Hate sets), not more PoG camping. Beholdiin can split time Hate vs PoG Bladesman’s.
