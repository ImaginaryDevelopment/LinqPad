# Deathknight — Poison Contagion Build

Soulstone Survivors (post-1.0). Primary clear/boss build focused on stacking Poison and detonating it with **Contagion**.

## Why this build

Deathknight can lean Doom or Poison. Poison wins here because:

- **Festering Strike** applies **Weakness**, which amplifies Poison ticks
- **Contagion** (prestige 20 unique) is a better kill skill than Pestilence — AoE around you, 250% poison-stack detonation, no aiming
- You cannot dash; Poison + big AoE lets you kite and still melt packs without precise targeting

## Character notes

| Trait | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Movement | +90% base; **cannot dash** |
| Area | +30% |
| Cast frequency | −10% |
| Extra | High body mass, immune to stun |

**Unlock:** Win Frozen Wastelands with curse tiers up through V, then spend 8 Violet Soulstones.

Playstyle is “lawnmower”: keep moving, drag poison trails / AoE through packs, let Contagion pop stacked Poison. Aim for roughly **200–250% movement speed** from passives + weapon (you stop needing more past that).

## Skills (final 6–7) — Poison Contagion

### Core (do not swap)

| Skill | Role |
| --- | --- |
| **Festering Strike** | Poison + Weakness applicator (weapon start) |
| **Contagion** | Poison detonate / Lord melt (P20) |
| **Path of Decay** | Poison trail while you ride (P50) |

Skip **Pestilence** once Contagion is online — Contagion covers the kill role better.

### Flex slots (2–3)

| Role | Prefer | Solid swaps (same role) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Sustained Poison AoE** | **Acid Rain** | Poison Cloud, Corrosion |
| **Extra applicator** | **Poison Bolt** | Poison Cloud, Pestilence *(only if Contagion not unlocked yet)* |
| **Finisher / 7th** | **Lethal Blade** *(if Weapon Expert)* | Corrosion, Bladestorm |

† Prefer native Nature Poison. **Skill Mastery: Nature / Ice / Shadow / Swing / Slam** — useless (native). Use **Inclination: Nature** if unlocked.

**Example final 6:** Festering Strike · Contagion · Path of Decay · Acid Rain · Poison Bolt · Lethal Blade  
**Example final 7 (Repertory):** above + Corrosion / Poison Cloud  
**Hands-off 6 (no Lethal Blade):** Festering Strike · Contagion · Path of Decay · Acid Rain · Poison Bolt · Corrosion

**Swap rules:**

- Never drop **Festering Strike** or **Contagion**.
- Prefer skills that apply **Poison** and/or **Weakness**.
- Dump Contagion when packs / Lords are heavily stacked — you do not need to aim.
- Do not fill with Doom / Bleed / Fire clutter on this path.

### Skill priority while leveling

**“Lock early”** = take immediately and keep for the run.

1. **Festering Strike** + **Contagion** first
2. **Acid Rain** / **Poison Bolt** / Corrosion
3. **Path of Decay** once offered (strong, but secondary to Contagion + Festering)
4. **Lethal Blade** only if you took Weapon Expert and like aiming — otherwise skip

## Passives — take / ban

### Prioritize

1. **Movement speed** until ~200–250%
2. **Poison** power, duration, application chance (**Venomous**, etc.)
3. **Multicast**, **cast frequency**, **area**
4. **Damage** / crit once Poison is rolling
5. **Magnetic** (pickup) for smoother leveling
6. Some **armor**, **max HP**, **block**, or flat DR once curses hit hard

### Banish

- Passives that push **Bleed**, **Fire**, or **Doom** as your main ailment
- Off-element clutter that dilutes Poison / Weakness focus

Physical + Poison is the plan. Weakness from Festering Strike (and Dragonflight if unlocked) is the multiplier you want to keep feeding.

## How to play a run

1. Start on Greatsword of Corruption → ride wide circles, never stand still.
2. Stack Poison on everything; Contagion when packs or lords are heavily stacked.
3. Path of Decay: cut through or beside packs so the trail stays useful; enemies follow you, so overlapping loops help.
4. Summon **Ebon Knights** at the Deathknight ritual when soul gauges fill — free pressure and distraction on lords.
5. Bosses: keep Weakness up, dump Contagion on high Poison stacks, keep moving through telegraph patterns (no dash means pathing discipline).
