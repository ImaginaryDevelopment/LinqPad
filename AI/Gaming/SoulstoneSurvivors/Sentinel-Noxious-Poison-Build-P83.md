## Why Noxious Poison (not Huntress / not Soulfrost)

At P83 the Sentinel ceiling is **Poison stack density from projectile spam**:

- **Noxious Shot** + **Venomous Volley** apply **Poison** (and Weakness on Volley) at high fire rate
- Class **+move / +dash / +crit** + Noxious **+HP / +move** → you corner-camp packs safely while Poison spreads
- **Pestilence** scales with Poison stacks for Lord delete
- **Venomous** + **Corrosive** passives turn every arrow into more Poison; **Infected Wounds** (crit → Poison) is grotesque with Aptitude crit chance
- **Soulfrost** is a craftable Ice 5th weapon — fine, but not the class peak
- Huntress Aptitude / Siege Bleed clear fine at mid prestige; they lose to Noxious Poison at max curse

## Passives — take / ban

### Prioritize

1. **Poison:** Venomous · Corrosive · Infected Wounds · Death and Decay
2. **Weakness** amplifiers (Venomous Volley already applies it)
3. **Cast frequency** → **Multicast**
4. **Critical chance** (especially with Infected Wounds) → crit damage
5. **Movement** + **Magnetic**
6. Flat **damage** (boosts skill + Poison)
7. Skill Chain: Poison → Projectile (if offered)

### Hard-ban / ban

- **Bleed** main-ailment passives (Hemorrhage focus)
- **Doom / Fire** as primary ailment paths
- **Leviathan** (move tax)
- Do not banish Venomous / Corrosive / cast-freq

**Immovable Object:** skip — you move constantly.

## How to play a run

1. Weapon Expert → **Venomous Volley**; start stacking Poison immediately.
2. Lock **Pestilence** + **Ricochet**; fill Acid Rain / Storm.
3. Grab **Venomous** early — every projectile becomes a Poison applicator.
4. Hunt **panthers** for mount; save mount for Lord waves when you can.
5. Corner-camp denser spawns if your dodge is clean — Poison spreads harder in clumps.
6. Lords: stack Poison → Pestilence + Death Touch explosions delete. Drive-by blue turrets with move speed, then return to camp.
