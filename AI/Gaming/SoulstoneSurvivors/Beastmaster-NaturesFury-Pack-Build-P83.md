## Why Nature’s Fury Pack (not Widow-first / not Cleaver)

At P83 the Beastmaster ceiling is **body count → Wild Strike + Harmony**:

- **Wild Strike** deals **+20% damage per active summon** — your entire kit exists to feed that number
- **Swamp Boar** (Weapon Expert) + **War Tiger** + **Battle Moose** + Call-of-the-Wild exotics + skill-tree wolves = permanent pack density
- **Bestial Wrath** is the class’s real buff — move + **multicast** on you and allies (pets cast more)
- Nature’s Fury weapon **−cast freq / +multicast** is a feature: pets live longer between recasts while you spam Wild Strike copies
- **Widow’s Embrace** Cocoon spam is a real high-curse contender — treat as sidegrade / Arsenal pull, not default gun
- **Wild Cleaver** Poison and **Bonecrusher** Body Slam are solid alts when you want less “zoo AI”

**Call of the Wild (ascension):** animal **tracks** appear on the map. Walk them in a chain to fill the gauge → temporary **exotic beast** pack (huge free DPS). Footprints give short move buffs from the tree — treat tracks like Engineer fuel / Shaman pyres. Skill-tree **wolves** are permanent companions once bought.

**Playstyle:** keep the pack up, follow tracks, face Lords while tigers (Cunning) and moose (Taunt) hold aggro. You are a summoner with modest HP — fix **−10% XP** with Magnetic / XP passives early; armor is optional.

**Swap rules:**

- Never drop the four **cores**.
- Prefer skills that **add summoned units** (feeds Wild Strike + Harmony).
- **Skill Mastery: Nature / Fire / Swing / Slam / Bomb** — useless (native). Use **Inclination: Nature** only if you pivot Poison.
- Empowering buffs (**Bestial Wrath**, Bloodlust) **refresh, do not stack** — overlap = duration refresh only.

## Passives — take / ban

### Prioritize

1. **Max summons** / summon damage / summon cast freq / duration
2. **Multicast** / cast frequency (Wild Strike hose + Wrath value)
3. **Movement** + **Magnetic** (tracks, XP, weapons — class is −10% XP)
4. Crit chance → crit damage (Wild Strike + Finesse)
5. **Call of the Wild** / exotic beast nodes when offered
6. Area (Boar / Moose clear)
7. Under Versatile Strength: one high-rarity pick per new passive **type**

### Soft-ban / ban

- Main-ailment **Doom / Ice** greed that dilutes the pack
- **Skill Mastery** for types you already have
- Don’t banish summon count / multicast / Magnetic
- Pure Fire Meteor greed soft-ban unless you purposely pivot Burn

## How to play a run

1. Weapon Expert → Boars + Wild Strike; lock **War Tiger** then **Bestial Wrath**.
2. Follow **tracks** whenever safe — exotic windows are free Lord DPS.
3. Keep pack refreshed; circle Lords so **Cunning** tigers stay glued to them; Moose **Taunt** buys space.
4. Pull **Spider Queen / Cocoon / Cave Bear** from Arsenal when offered — more bodies beat random Fire spam.
5. Try **Widow Cocoon**, **Cleaver Poison**, or **Bonecrusher Slam** as sidegrades; Nature’s Fury remains the peak pack kit.
