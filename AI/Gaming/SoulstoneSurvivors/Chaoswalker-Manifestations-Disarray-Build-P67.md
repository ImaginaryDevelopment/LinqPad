## Why Manifestations Disarray (not Azramiel / not pure Poison-Fire)

- **Azramiel’s Blessing** is prestige **70** — ignore Holy wand guides for now
- **Manifestations of Chaos** (P50) is the class identity spike: damage scales with **Disarray** stacks and has high **Chaotic** proc rate
- **Skull of Draxiz** + **Unchained Chaos** is the best Disarray applicator you own
- **Inclination: Chaos** (P60) finally biases the pool toward Chaos / Chaotic / Disarray skills
- Artifact / Flamewalker are fine **alts** if you want Poison or Burn instead of pure Chaos

**Skill types:** Chaos · Nature · Fire · Shadow · Holy

**Altar of Chaos (ascension):** kills drop chaotic essences → fill the gauge → activate a **Chaos Pillar** on the map. Pillars apply heavy **Disarray** (Fragments / Pillar / Vortex / Wall). Treat pillars like Shaman ritual pyres / Engineer fuel — go activate them.

**Playstyle:** kite (class is glass: −HP, −crystal heal, Skull −armor). Stack Disarray, let Chaotic chain-cast your kit, dump Manifestations on packed packs and Lords. Take some max HP / armor passives.

**Chaotic:** when a skill with Chaotic fires, it has a % chance to cast one of your *other* skills. Chaotic does **not** proc Chaotic again on the triggered skill — still a huge cast-rate multiplier.

**Disarray:** each stack adds a random damage-taken bonus (roughly −0.5% ↔ +2.5% per stack, soft/hard capped high). Multiplies with other amp debuffs. Manifestations deal more when stacks are high.

**Swap rules:**

- Never drop **Chaotic Missile**, **Unchained Chaos**, or **Manifestations**.
- Prefer skills that apply **Disarray** and/or have **Chaotic**.
- **Chaos Golem** Taunts — packs and Lords pile on the golem; skip if it gets you killed.
- **Skill Mastery: Chaos** is useless — you already have Chaos. Use **Inclination: Chaos**.
- Do not wait for Azramiel Holy skills.

## Passives — take / ban

### Prioritize

1. **“Apply X when you apply Y”** cross-ailment passives — Chaoswalker wants many unique statuses for Executioner + Disarray amp
2. **Cast frequency** / **multicast** / Chaotic helpers
3. **Disarray** / Chaos skill damage / area (class +20% area)
4. **Max HP** / armor (class −20 HP, −crystal heal, Skull −armor)
5. Crit chance → crit damage (class +10% crit)
6. Magnetic (essences + XP)

### Soft-ban / ban

- Don’t banish Disarray / Chaos / Chaotic paths
- Pure single-ailment greed (only Burn, only Doom) soft-bans the Chaoswalker fantasy unless you pivoted to Flamewalker / Shadow intentionally
- **Skill Mastery: Chaos / Nature / Fire / Shadow / Holy** — you already have those types

## How to play a run

1. Weapon Expert → Unchained Chaos + Chaotic Missile; lock **Manifestations** early.
2. Stack Disarray; let Chaotic chain your kit. Manifestations melt packed, stacked targets.
3. Pick up **essences** → activate **Chaos Pillars** whenever the gauge allows — free Disarray bombs.
4. Lords: keep Unchained / Totem / Bunnies applying stacks, then Manifestations.
5. Farm **prestige 70 Azramiel’s Blessing** for Holy; Artifact / Flamewalker anytime as Poison / Burn sidegrades.
