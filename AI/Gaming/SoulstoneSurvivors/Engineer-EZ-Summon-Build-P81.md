## Why EZ Summon peak (not Bomb Launcher-first)

At P81 the Engineer ceiling is **turret + spider density under Ammo Box**:

- **EZ Sentry** + **EZ Mechanical Spider** + **Ammo Box** + **Heavy Artillery** + Ballista is still the strongest “park and delete” kit
- Spiders apply **Shattered** — Lords melt while you scoop fuel for **Mechatron**
- **Bomb Launcher** as a *weapon* is mediocre (slow QT Bombuddy start, −HP, −cast freq). Prefer **EZ** + **Unexpected Arsenal** to pull **QT Land Surprise** / **QT Bombuddy** / **Guided Missiles** / **Energizer** without sitting on QT-22
- Active **Bleed + Shrapnel** (Ammo Devourer or QT-22 board) is a real alt for players who hate camping — see below
- **Volt Cannon** remains the Electric crit sidegrade

**Skill types:** Blast · Bomb · Earth · Electric · Projectile

**Mechatron OP-9001 (ascension):** coal/fuel drops from kills. Fill the gauge → transform: armor / block / move buffs + **missile barrage**. Pick up fuel like Hound Master meat. With a full tree, Mechatron windows are a major Lord spike — do not ignore fuel.

**Ammunition:** at 20 stacks, cast consumes them for +5 multicast charges. **Ammo Box** feeds you **and** nearby summons every second — stay in range.

**Playstyle:** claim a corner or choke, plant Ammo Box + sentries, let spiders shred armor. You are a general, not glass — armor / HP passives are fine. Fix **−10% move**.

## Passives — take / ban

### Prioritize

1. **Max summons** / summon damage / summon cast freq
2. **Multicast** / cast frequency / Ammunition helpers
3. **Bleed** (Bloodshed, Brutal Strikes, etc.) + Weakness
4. **Movement** (class −10%) + **Magnetic** (fuel + XP)
5. **Armor** / max HP (Mechatron loves defense)
6. Crit chance → crit damage (class +crit damage; Dazed from Surge helps)
7. **Mechatron** nodes when offered

### Soft-ban / ban

- Main-ailment **Poison / Fire / Doom** greed that dilutes Bleed
- **Skill Mastery** for types you already have
- Don’t banish summon count / Ammunition / Bleed paths

## How to play a run

1. Weapon Expert → spiders + EZ Sentry; lock **Ammo Box** early.
2. Plant Ammo Box in your fight zone; layer Heavy Artillery + Ballista.
3. Scoop **fuel** → Mechatron windows for tankiness + missiles on Lords.
4. Lords: spiders on them (Shattered), stay in Ammo Box range so turrets multicast; optional Shrapnel / QT Bombuddy when stacks are thick.
5. Try **Ammo Devourer Bleed**, **Volt Electric**, or QT-22 as a gun for fun — EZ remains the peak camp kit.
