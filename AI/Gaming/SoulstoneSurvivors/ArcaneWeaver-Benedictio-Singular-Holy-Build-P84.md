# Arcane Weaver — Benedictio Singular Holy Build (Prestige 84)

Soulstone Survivors. Tuned for **prestige 84** — **maximum Arcane Weaver power before Legendary**. **Benedictio, Staff of Dawn** Singular **Light Beam** is the ceiling. **Pandemonium** is the P100 Legendary — see [`ArcaneWeaver-Pandemonium-Disarray-Build-P100.md`](ArcaneWeaver-Pandemonium-Disarray-Build-P100.md). Glacies Ice is the prestige-70 unlock and is **not** the peak.

## Why Benedictio Singular Holy (not Glacies / not Arcane Staff / not Pandemonium-yet)

At P84 you **cannot** craft **Pandemonium** — Legendary weapons unlock at **prestige 100**.

Best available kit:

- **Light Beam** ×4–5 stacks **Exposed** (percent damage amp) while you aim at Lords
- **Celestial Retribution** scales hard off Exposed — Lord delete button
- Benedictio stats: **+crit chance / +area / +multicast** (pays for −HP)
- **Mana Infusion** Infused multicast multiplies overlapping beams
- **Glacies** (P70) is the prestige unlock and the **worst** staff for high curse
- **Ignis** Burn is a solid Fire alt, not the ceiling
- Singular Arcane Beam is the same idea with weaker **Brittle** flat amp — Holy wins

**Playstyle:** glass laser pointer — keep beams on Lords, kite, scoop mana. Take HP / armor when offered. Do not greed Glacies “because P70.”

## Skills (final 6–7) — Benedictio Singular Holy

### Core (do not swap)

| Skill | Count | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Light Beam** | **4–5×** | Exposed stack + primary DPS |
| **Celestial Retribution** | **1×** | Scales off Exposed — Lord melt (Weapon Expert) |

Needs **Singular Focus**. With **Improved Repertory**: Light Beam ×5 + Retribution ×1 + buff, or Light Beam ×4 + Retribution + Arcane Power.

### Flex / early fillers (before duplicates land)

| Role | Prefer | Solid swaps (same role) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Multicast buff** | **Arcane Power** | Bloodlust |
| **Extra Exposed bridge** | Keep early Sacred Orb briefly | Drop for Beam copies |
| **Clear (only if beams fail)** | Arcane Conjuration | Skip once Beams fill |

**Example final 6:** Light Beam ×4 · Celestial Retribution · Arcane Power  
**Example final 7 (Repertory):** Light Beam ×5 · Celestial Retribution · Arcane Power *(or Beam ×5 + Retribution if you drop the buff)*

**Swap rules:**

- Never drop below **3× Light Beam** and **1× Celestial Retribution** once Singular Focus is online.
- **Banish other Holy junk** (except Retribution) until Beam copies are filled.
- **Skill Mastery: Holy / Arcane / Fire / Ice / Chaos** — useless (native). Use **Inclination: Holy**.
- Skip Glacies / Frost Beam / Ice Shield on this path.

### Skill priority while leveling

1. **Unconventional Start** → ditch Sacred Orb ASAP
2. **Celestial Retribution** (Weapon Expert) — keep
3. First **Light Beam** — lock; take every duplicate
4. Arcane Power while waiting for Beams
5. Fill to 4–5 Beams; drop fillers

## Passives — take / ban

### Prioritize

1. **Multicast** (overlapping Beams)
2. **Cast frequency**
3. Flat **damage** / Lasting skill damage
4. **Magnetic** + **Movement** (stay on Lords + crystals)
5. **Max HP** / armor (Benedictio is −HP)
6. Crit chance → crit damage (weapon already stacks crit chance)
7. Celestial Retribution skill power-ups when offered

### Hard-ban / ban

- Pure **Ice** / Glacies paths
- Random Holy that is **not** Light Beam / Retribution (banish them)
- **Leviathan**
- Do not banish Exposed / multicast / Magnetic

## How to play a run

1. Unconventional Start → open toward Light Beam / Arcane Power.
2. Lock **Celestial Retribution**; stack **Light Beam** copies; banish other Holy.
3. **Scoop Mana Crystals** — Infused multicast = more overlapping lasers.
4. Point all Beams at Lords; Retribution deletes when Exposed is high.
5. Respect telegraphs — paper mage. Dash, don’t tank.
6. At **P100**, craft **Pandemonium** and switch to the Disarray guide.
