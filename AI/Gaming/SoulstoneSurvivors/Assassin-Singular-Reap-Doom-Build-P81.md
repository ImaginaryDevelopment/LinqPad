## Why Singular Reap (not Bleed / not Swing / not multi-skill Doom)

At P81 the Assassin ceiling is **Reap under Ambush**:

- **Reap** applies huge **Doom** stacks; **Ambush** detonates **all Doom in the area at 250%** instantly
- **Singular Focus** + **Focused Mind** → stack **multiple Reaps** (+70% damage) — community peak builds run **4–5× Reap** and delete Lords at max curse
- **Bounty Hunter** mark kill grants **Stealth** → every hit counts as Ambush without Smoke Bomb or positioning
- Bleed Ambush (Worn Daggers) and Swing spam (Curved) are fine mid-game; they fall off hard vs stacked Doom detonation
- **Shadow Step** is actively harmful here (random teleports, Reap whiffs)

## Passives — take / ban

### Prioritize

1. **Doom:** Calamity · Ruin · Fateful Strikes · Grim Precision
2. **Bounty Hunter** (both nodes if offered) — mark uptime = permanent Ambush windows
3. **Cast frequency** → push Reap CD toward ~0.2s (endgame target per high-curse runners)
4. **Multicast** on Reap
5. **Movement** + **Magnetic**
6. Rare **armor / max HP** (Edge −armor, Demonic Pact −armor, glass everywhere)

### Hard-ban / ban

- **Bleed** passives (Hemorrhage, Laceration, Poisonous Blood) — wrong ailment
- **Poison / Fire** main-ailment passives
- **Shadow Step** skill offers
- **Leviathan** (move tax)
- Do not banish Doom / Shadow / cast-freq paths

**Elemental Flow:** skip for pure Reap — Doom detonates instantly under Ambush; Flow helps DoT ticks, not your kill pattern.

## How to play a run

1. Weapon Expert → **Reap**; reroll until duplicate Reap (Singular Focus online).
2. Drop **Shadow Stab** once you have 2+ Reaps.
3. Hunt **Marked Targets** — save one for each Lord wave when possible; Stealth = free Ambush on every Reap swing.
4. Optional: **Smoke Bomb** under a Lord until Bounty Stealth is up.
5. Stand in red circles at your own risk — you are glass. Dash through telegraphs.
6. Lords: stack Doom with Reap spam → Ambush detonate deletes. No bleed setup, no enclave babysitting.
