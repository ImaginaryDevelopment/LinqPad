## Why Bleed + Ambush (not Doom)

Popular Assassin guides assume **Edge of Doom** + **Reap**. You do not have that weapon yet.

Instead:

- **Worn Daggers** → **Eviscerate** under Ambush scales with Bleed stacks (your kill skill)
- **Smoke Bomb** makes every hit inside the cloud count as Ambush (no need to stay behind enemies)
- **Summon Assassin Enclave** adds many Bleed-applying minions
- Bombs (**Fan of Bombs**, **Shrapnel Bomb**) clear packs and scale with Bleed

Glass-cannon kite class: use dashes + move speed; do not tank.

**Ambush** = hit from behind, *or* hit while you/the target are under Smoke Bomb / Bounty Stealth. Skills with Ambush tags get their bonus effect (extra Bleed, burst, etc.).

**Bounty Hunter:** when a Marked Target appears, kill it for a big temporary buff package (crit, damage, cast freq, move, multicast, and **Stealth** so all hits Ambush). Hold the mark until a Lord if you can, then cash it in for the boss fight.

### Skill priority while leveling

**“Lock early”** = when these show as level-up choices, **take them immediately** and keep them in a skill slot for the rest of the run (do not skip/reroll them hoping for something better).

1. **Smoke Bomb** and **Eviscerate** first (Ambush engine + kill skill)
2. Then **Sinister Strike** (keep if you started with it) + **Fan of Bombs** / **Shrapnel Bomb**
3. **Summon Assassin Enclave** when offered
4. Fill last slot with Bloody Saw / Flurry / Debilitating Bomb

Drop Rupture if it appears — it is not worth a slot here.

## Passives — take / ban

### Prioritize

1. **Bleed** power, stacks, tick rate, Hemorrhage / Bloodshed / Laceration / Brutal Strikes (Bleed on crit)
2. **Cast frequency** and **multicast** (more Bleed applications)
3. **Critical chance** toward 100%, then crit damage
4. **Movement speed** + use your extra dash
5. **Magnetic** for smoother leveling
6. Some **max HP** / armor (weapons cut defense)

### Banish

- Passives that push **Poison**, **Fire**, or **Doom** as your main ailment
- **Poisonous Blood** (Bleed → Poison) — see why below
- **Leviathan** (move-speed tax) unless you are already very fast

**Why ban Poisonous Blood?** It does not “add free damage” in a vacuum. It converts or rides on Bleed into Poison, which **dilutes** this build because:

1. **Kill skills read Bleed stacks.** Eviscerate (Ambush) and Shrapnel Bomb scale with how much **Bleed** is on the target. If stacks are turned into Poison (or you’re splitting pressure across two ailments), those skills fire with a smaller Bleed number → less burst.
2. **Your passives boost Bleed, not Poison.** You take Laceration / Hemorrhage / Bleed-on-crit / Elemental Flow for Bleed ticks. Poison from Poisonous Blood does not get that full package, so converted stacks are weaker than leaving them as Bleed.
3. **Level-up pool pollution.** Once Poison is in the mix, the game offers more Poison passives. Those compete with the Bleed/crit/cast-freq choices you actually want.

Net: same hits, less of the resource your detonators care about, and worse passive offers. Ban it unless you are intentionally going hybrid Bleed+Poison (this guide is not).

## How to play a run

1. Drop **Smoke Bomb** on packs / under Lords; stand in the cloud so every skill Ambush-procs.
2. Stack Bleed with Sinister Strike, bombs, and Enclave minions.
3. **Eviscerate** (and Shrapnel Bomb) when stacks are high — that is your burst.
4. Dash through telegraphs; you are fragile.
5. Complete **Bounty Hunter** marks for Stealth + stat buffs; save one for Lord spawns when possible.
