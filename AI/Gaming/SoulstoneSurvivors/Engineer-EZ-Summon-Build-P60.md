You have **~40 skill-tree points**. Prioritize **Mechatron OP-9001**, then Mechatron potency / cast freq / multicast / armor. Buy Engineer runes: **Extended Magazines** (1), **Quick Reload** (1), **Armor-piercing Rounds** (2).

## Why EZ Summon (not Bomb Launcher / not minigun-only)

- **Bomb Launcher** is prestige **70** — ignore QT Bombuddy guides for now
- **EZ Sentry** + **EZ Mechanical Spider** + **Ammo Box** is the Engineer fantasy: park turrets, spiders shred armor (**Shattered**), Ammunition makes everything multicast
- **Heavy Artillery** adds Bleed + Weakness without needing the Bomb Launcher
- **Volt Cannon** is a fine Electric crit alt if you hate standing near Ammo Boxes
- Class **+15% XP** makes prestige farming pleasant; fix **−10% move** with passives

**Mechatron OP-9001 (ascension):** coal/fuel drops from kills. Fill the gauge → transform: armor / block / move buffs + **missile barrage**. Pick up fuel like Hound Master meat.

**Playstyle:** claim a corner or choke, plant **Ammo Box** + sentries, let spiders follow you when you reposition. You are more “general” than glass kite — armor passives are fine.

**Ammunition:** at 20 stacks, cast consumes them for +5 multicast charges. Ammo Box feeds you **and** nearby summons every second — stay in range when you can.

## Passives — take / ban

### Prioritize

1. **Max summons** / summon damage / summon cast freq
2. **Multicast** / cast frequency / Ammunition helpers
3. **Bleed** (Venomous-equivalents: Bloodshed, Brutal Strikes, etc.) + Weakness
4. **Movement** (class −10%) + Magnetic (fuel + XP)
5. **Armor** / max HP (Mechatron loves defense)
6. Crit chance → crit damage (class has +crit damage, low crit chance — Dazed helps)

### Soft-ban / ban

- Main-ailment **Poison / Fire / Doom** if it dilutes Bleed
- Don’t banish summon count / Ammunition / Bleed paths

## How to play a run

1. Weapon Expert → spiders + EZ Sentry; lock **Ammo Box** early.
2. Plant Ammo Box in your fight zone; layer Heavy Artillery + Ballista.
3. Pick up **fuel** → Mechatron windows for tankiness + missiles.
4. Lords: keep spiders on them (Shattered), stay in Ammo Box range so turrets multicast.
5. Farm **prestige 70 Bomb Launcher** if you want a Bomb/Bleed pivot; Volt Cannon anytime for Electric fun.
