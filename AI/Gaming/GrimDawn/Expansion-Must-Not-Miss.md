# Grim Dawn — Expansion Must-Not-Miss

Permanent bonuses, devotion, system unlocks, and easy-to-skip content from **Ashes of Malmouth (AoM)**, **Forgotten Gods (FG)**, and **Crucible**. Base-game permanent point quests are included because Merit tokens and Ultimate-only expansion rewards assume you know the full checklist.

**Live tracker:** [Grimtools Important Quests (Ultimate)](https://www.grimtools.com/checklist/quests/ultimate) — use this while playing.

## Permanent skill & attribute points (the real checklist)

These are **extra** points beyond leveling. Miss them and the character is permanently weaker.

### Base game (all 3 difficulties — do every difficulty, or use Merits)

| Quest | Where | Reward (per difficulty) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Kasparov’s Experiment** | Kasparov, Devil’s Crossing | **+1 skill** |
| **A Merciful End** | Minova (ghost), Broken Hills / undercity exit area | **+1 attribute** |
| **The Hidden Path** (3rd shrine / mid chain) | Kymon’s / Death’s Vigil secret path | **+1 attribute** |
| **The Hidden Path** (Sentinel / Attendant finale) | End of Hidden Path | **+1 skill** |

**Totals if done on Normal + Elite + Ultimate:** **+6 skill**, **+6 attribute**.

### Ashes of Malmouth

| Quest | Notes | Reward |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The Other You** | Seer Rugia @ Coven’s Refuge — needs **Honored** with Coven + Living Factory progress. Fight Allostria behind the Living Flesh door in **The Infestation** | **+1 skill on Ultimate only** |

AoM also raises the level cap to **100** (more level-up skill/attribute points than base).

### Forgotten Gods

| Quest | Notes | Reward |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Tomb of the Eldritch Sun** → speak to **Uroboruuk** | Main FG story beat | **+1 skill** (Ultimate) |
| **The Messenger** | Return to Seer Laurna | **+1 attribute** (Ultimate) |
| **Entering the Shattered Realm** | Reach **Shard 5** via Mazaan | **+1 skill** |
| **Exploring the Shattered Realm** | Next SR checkpoint quest | **+1 skill** |
| **Conquering the Shattered Realm** | Later SR checkpoint | **+1 skill + +1 attribute** |

**Do not skip talking to Mazaan** at the Conclave once you’ve joined a cult — Shattered Realm quests are easy to never start.

### Skip Normal/Elite the smart way (FG)

Buy **Merit tokens** at the Forgotten Gods hub (Conclave):

| Token | What it grants |
| --- | --- |
| **Champion’s Merit** | Unlocks **Elite** + all Normal skill/attribute quest rewards + bags + riftgates |
| **Saviour’s Merit** | Unlocks **Ultimate** + all Normal **and** Elite quest rewards + bags + riftgates |

Put a token in shared stash → new/alt character claims it early (Burial Hill unlock or via Crucible stash access). Ideal for alts once one character has reached FG.

## Devotion (55-cap systems)

| Fact | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Cap | **50** base → **55** with AoM |
| How you earn | Restore **shrines** (1 point each until cap) |
| After cap | Shrines still give XP + loot, no more points |
| FG change | Extra shrines + **all shrines available on Ultimate** → you can hit **55/55 in one Ultimate playthrough** |

### Easy-to-miss devotion content

1. **Rhowari Legacy / Mogdrogen shrine** (Asterkarn Valley)  
   - Requires **Honored Rovers** → quest from Amkala (Old Arkovia).  
   - Collect Runed Stone + Spirit of Mogdrogen (Ashen Waste / Forsaken Wastes rifts) + Ancient Heart.  
   - Restores a devotion shrine **and** unlocks **Avatar of Mogdrogen** (farmable celestial / unique drops).

2. **AoM / FG area shrines** — don’t speed-run past ruined/desecrated shrines in Ugdenbog, Malmouth, Korvan Basin. If under 55, every shrine matters.

3. **Crucible tribute → devotion** (if you own Crucible)  
   - Talk to **Torralia** → “prove my devotion.”  
   - Cheap early (first points ~3 tribute each), expensive later.  
   - Tribute can buy up to **50**; the last **5** still need campaign shrines.  
   - Best used on **fresh alts** (buy ~5–10 early), not on a 90 with 40 shrine points already.

## System unlocks you should never skip

| Unlock | Expansion | How | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Inventor — component remove** | Base | First inventor quests | Salvage rare components without destroying the item |
| **Inventor — Dismantle** | Base | **Dangerous Curiosity** (Kasparov; Respected Devil’s Crossing + dynamite) | Break yellow+ gear into scrap/components with dynamite |
| **Inventor — Set Transmute** | **FG** | **The Tome of Eanatum** (Kargon @ Conclave → Sunbane Oasis corpse) | Turn duplicate set pieces into other pieces of the **same set** — critical for farming sets like Spellscourge |
| **Shattered Realm** | **FG** | **Loyalty to the Three** → talk to **Mazaan** | Best endgame legendary volume + SR skill/attr quests + rune augments / Shattered Guardian set |
| **Merit tokens** | **FG** | Buy at Conclave hub | Alt / Ultimate-skip pipeline |
| **Potion of Clarity** (and similar) | **AoM** | Malmouth Resistance vendors / reputation | Movement speed potion — huge QoL while leveling & farming |

Transmute cost reminder: polished emerald + aether crystals + **Eldritch Essence** (FG material). Unlock the quest early in FG; don’t wait until you’re drowning in duplicate helms.

## Faction choices that lock content

### Barrowholm (AoM) — Friendly vs Hostile

| Choice | You get | You lose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Friendly** (usual recommendation) | Faction shop, bounties, augments, life-leech potions, **Ravager** (celestial), Feast-related spawn help | Reaper path / Forlorn Cellar route |
| **Hostile** | Fight Barrowholm, **Namadea / Reaper of the Lost**, extra cellar dungeon, some cannibal MIs | Ravager + Barrowholm shop/augments on that difficulty’s choice path |

You can choose differently per difficulty, but reputation state is shared across difficulties — plan Ultimate carefully. For a first character: **go Friendly**.

### Cult of the Three (FG) — Bysmiel / Dreeg / Solael

- All three stay **friendly**; picking one does **not** lock the others’ vendors.
- Your pick gets faster rep from quests; each cult has unique Honored quests / **movement skill augment** recipes.
- **Optimal:** pick a different cult on Normal / Elite / Ultimate (or across alts) so one account unlocks all cult recipes.

### AoM factions to push to Revered (gear, not just lore)

| Faction | Why |
| --- | --- |
| **Coven of Ugdenbog** | Strong rings/medals, chaos/pierce armor augments, gates **The Other You** |
| **Malmouth Resistance** | Top weapon/jewelry augments, tank armor, potions |
| **Barrowholm** (if friendly) | Dark-themed gear + strong niche augments |

### Base-game choices still relevant in expansions

- **Kymon’s Chosen vs Order of Death’s Vigil** — mutually exclusive friendly factions; do opposite choices on alts/difficulties for all recipes.
- **Outcast** — optional hostility for nemesis farming vs keeping him friendly for services.

## Expansion content worth deliberately visiting

### Ashes of Malmouth

- **Coven’s Refuge hub** — invent/spirit guide access, bounties, The Other You chain.
- **Steelcap District** — Malmouth endgame hub; inventor **Nadia**, quartermaster when Resistance is advanced.
- **Celestials / farm bosses unlocked by choices** — Ravager (friendly Barrowholm), etc.
- **One-shot “Exalted Stash” chests** in AoM zones (once per difficulty) — free epic/legendary lottery; don’t leave zones without checking known spots (Malmouth Harbor Victor’s stash via quest, Infestation fleshwarped stash, etc.).

### Forgotten Gods

- **Conclave of the Three** — single hub with nearly every service; buy Merits, start SR, do Kargon’s transmute quest immediately.
- **Shattered Realm** — permanent point quests + best set/legendary farm once stable.
- **Cult Honored quests** — movement augments and unique rewards; don’t ignore bounty boards.
- **Korvan / oasis shrines** — devotion if still under 55.

### Crucible (standalone DLC)

- Early devotion purchase for alts.
- Dense loot practice arena; tributes also buy temporary Celestial Blessings (not permanent).
- Merit-like difficulty unlocks exist in Crucible separately from FG Merits — still use FG Merits for campaign skip.

## Practical “don’t brick this character” order

For a character heading into endgame (like Nytemare at 90+):

1. Confirm **55/55 devotion** (or buy remaining via shrines / early Crucible plan on alts).
2. Confirm **all permanent point quests** on Ultimate (grimtools checklist) — especially **The Other You**, **FG Messenger / Tomb**, and **all three Shattered Realm** milestones.
3. Unlock **Tome of Eanatum** (set transmute) before heavy set farming.
4. Keep **Coven Honored** long enough to finish **The Other You**.
5. Prefer **Friendly Barrowholm** unless you specifically need Reaper MIs this difficulty.
6. Rotate FG cults across difficulties/alts for all movement augments.
7. Buy **Merits** for future alts once FG hub is open.
