## DEE vs Bloody Pox (why this file exists)

| | **Dreeg’s Evil Eye (this build)** | **Bloody Pox** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Feel** | Artillery caster — aim, bombard, explode | Plague spreader — tag pack, walk away |
| **Clear (trash)** | Strong AoE if you keep casting | Usually **faster / lazier** — contagion does the work |
| **Bosses / ST** | **Clearly better** — direct damage | Weaker — DoTs tick slowly |
| **Survival** | Glass WH; more face-time while casting | **Easier for most players** — damage while kiting |
| **APM** | Higher (DEE + Curse + Sigil) | Lower once Pox is out |
| **Exploding fantasy** | **Yes** (Blood Burst / Vile Eruption / DEE mods) | Soft DoT cloud, not fireworks |

**Pick DEE** if you want casting fireworks and better bosses.  
**Pick Pox** if you want chill clear and safer kite play.  
**Hybrid is fine:** keep 1-pt+ Pox for trash OA shred while DEE stays the main button (Belladonna / Witch Queen style).

**Blood of Dreeg** is a **buff/heal**, not the damage engine — always up, never “main skill.”  
**Amarasta’s Blade Burst** is optional filler / Lethal Assault — not the caster identity here.

## Core idea

1. **DEE** is the engine — hard-cap DEE + damage modifiers (**Focused Gaze**, **Blood Burst**, **Terrifying Gaze**, **Vile Eruption** as points allow).
2. Convert everything useful to **acid/poison** (leveling: **Leafmane Horn**; endgame: set/weapons that finish vit→acid and feed DEE).
3. **Curse of Frailty → Vulnerability** = RR + DA shred — keep it on packs.
4. **Sigil of Consumption** = stand-in sustain / extra damage when packs stack on you.
5. **Blood of Dreeg** + **Pneumatic Burst** = permanent buffs (heal, OA/DA, regen, speed). Refresh; don’t treat them as DPS buttons.
6. **Possession** = absorb + % acid/poison — big defensive layer for a caster WH.
7. **Aspect of the Guardian** (Blood of Dreeg node) = % acid/poison + phys res — prioritize when unlocked.
8. Nightblade gives **Pneumatic Burst**, utility, and optional **Ring of Steel** / light melee fillers — you are still a **caster**, not SS.

Play loop: Curse → drop Sigil if needed → **spam DEE** into the pack → kite with movement rune → refresh BoD / PB when they drop.

## Skill priorities (Barbie at 46 → 100)

Soft-respec at Spirit Guide is cheap at 46. Peel anything invested in Shadow Strike / Dual Blades WPS if you were melee — put it into the DEE package.

| Phase | Spend on |
| --- | --- |
| **Now (46)** | Cap / near-cap **DEE** + **Focused Gaze** + **Blood Burst**. **Curse of Frailty** + start **Vulnerability**. **Blood of Dreeg** climbing. 1-pt **Sigil**. **Pneumatic Burst** soft-cap path. Occultist bar high enough for Possession / Aspect when available. |
| **46–60** | Finish **Vulnerability**. Max **Blood of Dreeg** → **Aspect of the Guardian**. Raise **Possession**. Push DEE modifiers (**Terrifying Gaze**, then **Vile Eruption**). Keep PB up. Light **Solael’s Witchfire** if energy/DA needed. |
| **60–80** | Cap Possession / Aspect. Add **Bloody Pox** (1–several points) as trash helper + OA shred — optional hybrid. Nightblade utility (Veil / Phantasmal Armor) as resists demand. |
| **80–100** | Mirror your chosen GT (Witch Queen / Belladonna). Drop dead weight. Energy regen + resist caps over vanity %damage. |

**Every-fight cheat sheet**

1. Blood of Dreeg + Pneumatic Burst **up**
2. Curse of Frailty on pack
3. Sigil under feet / on pack
4. Spam DEE
5. Movement rune out when surrounded
6. Optional: Bloody Pox tick on dense trash

## Playstyle notes (clear & survival)

### Clear
- Aim DEE through dense packs; let **Blood Burst / Vile Eruption** finish clumps.
- Curse early so RR is up before the second DEE volley.
- Optional Pox on huge trash pulls if you invested points.
- **Worse than Pox for lazy map clear**; **better than Pox** when something has a health bar and a name.

### Survival
- You are **not** a facetank. Surrounded = dead.
- Sigil + BoD heal + PB heal + Basilisk leech = your layers.
- Possession absorb is mandatory late — don’t skip it for vanity DPS.
- Cap resists (chaos/poison/aether especially in FG/AoM). Overcap for shred.
- Movement rune is a **skill**, not a luxury — bind it somewhere you will press.

### Expected ceiling (honest)
- Campaign / Ultimate farming: comfortable once Leafmane + resists online
- Mid SR / celestials: doable with kite discipline (Witch Queen reports Ravager / Mogdrogen with patience)
- Deep SR race / Cruc speed: not the point of this fantasy — SS acid WH or meta farmers are faster
