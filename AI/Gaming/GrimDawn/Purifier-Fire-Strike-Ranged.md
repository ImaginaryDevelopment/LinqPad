# Purifier Fire Strike — Ranged Nuker (Co-op Backline)

**Purifier** = Demolitionist + Inquisitor. Dual-pistol (or gun+shield) **Fire Strike** gunner — stand back, plant Seal / mines / Word of Pain, hold LMB, delete packs while someone else holds aggro.

**Designed to pair with:** a frontliner / sponge (Warder tank-support, DE Reaper mid-line, War Cry tank, etc.).

Primary sources (patch **1.2.1.6 → 1.3**):

- Leveling + endgame: [RSnoomak — Gun and Shield Purifier](https://forums.crateentertainment.com/t/gun-and-shield-purifier-leveling-guide-and-endgame-setup-1-2-1-6-1-3/154511)
- Endgame set paths: **Stronghold of the Legion** (currently exploding in power — mad_lee / guide callouts) and classic **Dagallon’s** DW Fire Strike
- RektbyProtoss Stronghold / Dagallon showcases (search grimtools author + Purifier)

Open grimtools from those threads — verify live.

## Why this for “I’ll keep things busy”

| Need | Purifier answer |
| --- | --- |
| Real **ranged** nuker | Guns — not melee pretending to be ranged |
| Top **scaling** | Fire Strike + WPS (Bursting Round / Storm Spread / etc.) scales into Stronghold / Dagallon endgame |
| Complementary to a tank | She plants **Inquisitor Seal**, drops RR, shoots; you hold faces |
| Solo OK when alone | Seal + Blast Shield + kiting — not helpless |
| Damage type vs cold DE / lightning Warder | **Fire** — separate RR package, no fighting over the same conversion gear |

Meta note: casts often beat pure ranged AA in deep SR, but among **gunners**, Purifier Fire Strike is the long-standing top scaling pick, and Stronghold is getting a huge spotlight into 1.3.

## Playstyle (backline)

1. Buffs up: **Word of Renewal**, **Deadly Aim** / relevant toggles, **Aura of Censure** (ele RR + safety).
2. Drop **Inquisitor Seal** at your feet (absorb bubble — stand in it).
3. Toss **Word of Pain** on the pack (long-duration AoE DoT + soft CC / tag).
4. Drop **Thermite Mine** (fire RR — huge for her damage).
5. Optional: Flashbang / Demon Fire / other Demo utility as skilled.
6. Hold **Fire Strike** (LMB) — WPS procs do the fireworks.

**Co-op:** You (tank) stand in the pack. She stands behind Seal and melts whatever you’re holding.  
**Solo:** Same loop; she uses Seal + range to not get eaten.

Low targeting compared to skillshot casters — mostly “drop circles, shoot forward.”

## What she brings (and what you cover)

| She provides | You provide (frontline) |
| --- | --- |
| Raw ranged DPS | Aggro / War Cry / channel body-block |
| **Thermite Mine** fire RR | Any cold/vit/lightning RR you already have |
| **Aura of Censure** ele RR + damage taken cut | Keep enemies off her Seal |
| **Word of Pain** pack softener | Stay near so she doesn’t have to reposition constantly |
| Seal self-peel if something slips | Primary sponge |

Different damage type = both of you amp different resists. That’s good in co-op.

## Leveling sketch

| Phase | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| **1–20** | Inquisitor first is common — **Word of Pain** carries early (guns feel bad until key crafts). Or follow RSnoomak’s gun+shield brackets. |
| **~15–25** | Craft / find pistols; start **Fire Strike**; rush WPS toward meaningful proc chance. |
| **Blacksmith** | Guide often wants **Duncan** for Double-Barrel pistol crafts (check live guide — differs from Angrim-for-caster advice). |
| **20–50** | Demo bar for Fire Strike / Thermite; Inquisitor Seal + Renewal online. |
| **50–100** | Cap RR tools, Seal, Fire Strike line; farm fire pistols / faction / greens → set pieces. |

**Attributes:** mostly **Physique**; enough Cunning for guns; Spirit as needed for energy/gear.

Early pain point (honest): levels **1–15** on guns can feel rough — Word of Pain carries; don’t judge the build there.

## Pairing notes

### With Warder tank-support / PS hybrid
Almost perfect MMO duo: Warder auras + War Cry in melee, Purifier Seal + guns behind. Squad Tactics cast/attack speed helps her fire rate.

### With Cold DE Reaper
You channel mid/front; she guns from Seal. Fire + cold RR both land. Don’t stack on the same tile if AoE ground effects overlap awkwardly — slightly staggered is fine as long as auras/Seal still matter.

### What not to pair into
Two glass backliners with nobody holding aggro. This file assumes **you** are the occupied-zone person.
