## Respec: how hard is it at higher level?

Short answer: **skill tweaks stay easy; identity changes do not.** Cost scales with how many points you refund, not with character level directly — but high-level characters have more points, so full board rebuilds hit the expensive tiers.

### What you can and cannot change

| Thing | How | Scales with level? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Skill points** (skills / modifiers) | Spirit Guide (Devil’s Crossing, Homestead, etc.) | Cost per refund rises as you refund more points in a session (tiers every ~20 points), **cap ~15,000 iron bits / point** |
| **Mastery bar points** | **Cannot refund** | N/A — this is the real lock |
| **Mastery choice** (e.g. Soldier → Occultist) | **Impossible** on that character | New character only |
| **Attribute points** | **Tonic of Reshaping** (quest / Nemesis) | Potion gate, not bit cost |
| **Devotion** | Spirit Guide (point-by-point) or **Tonic of Clarity** (full wipe) | Point-by-point gets expensive; tonic is the clean high-level option |

### Soft respec vs hard respec

**Soft (same masteries, new skill board)** — e.g. Nytemare stays Battlemage but swaps Cadence ↔ Forcewave, or moves Arcanist points around:

- Always available at a Spirit Guide.
- Early refunds are cheap; after you’ve peeled dozens of points you’re paying thousands per point, then the **15k cap**.
- A mid-level alt (28–46) can rebuild the whole skill tree for pocket change.
- A **92** with a full board can still do it, but a **full** wipe is a real iron-bits bill (hundreds of thousands if you’re near/at the cap for many points). Sell loot / farm a bit — annoying, not blocking.
- You still **cannot** pull points out of the mastery bars themselves. If you over-invested the Soldier bar to unlock a tier you no longer want, those bar points are stuck.

**Hard (different class fantasy)** — e.g. turn Battlemage into Blademaster or Warlock:

- **Not possible.** Masteries are permanent.
- Make a new character (or accept a suboptimal hybrid on the same dual masteries).

### Devotion / attributes at high level

- **Devotion:** experimenting at 90+ is where Tonics of Clarity matter. Refunding a deep constellation chain point-by-point at the Spirit Guide is slow and spendy; a tonic is one clean wipe.
- **Attributes:** same story with Tonic of Reshaping — one potion, full redistribute. Don’t treat attributes as freely mutable without stockpiling tonics (or Nemesis farming).

### Practical takeaway

1. **Experiment freely on alts under ~50** — respec is basically free.
2. On **Nytemare**, treat major skill reshuffles as a **budgeted** expense (or peel only the branches you’re changing).
3. Never expect to **change masteries** — pick dual classes carefully; alts are for other combos.
4. Stock **Tonic of Clarity / Reshaping** before big endgame devotion or attribute experiments.
