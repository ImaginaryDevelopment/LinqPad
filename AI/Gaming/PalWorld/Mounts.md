# Mounts — Speed, Stamina & Saddles

Index of rideable Pals by **sprint speed** (or swim dash for water). Stats from **paldb.cc 1.0** datamine. **Wild min** = lowest documented wild / Alpha level (PalMods / Game8); region spawns often go higher. `—` = breeding-only, raid-only, or no confirmed wild floor.

**1.0 naming:** some Cryst forms were renamed (e.g. **Ice Kingpaca**, **Ice Reptyro**). Dex numbers also shifted (see [Breeding-Sheet.md](./Breeding-Sheet.md)).

**Sprint** is what matters while holding sprint. **Stamina** is how long you can keep it up (flying falls when empty). Water mounts do **not** drain stamina while swimming.

## Speed passives (stack on any mount)

| Passive | Bonus | Notes |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Swift | +30% | Best; breedable |
| Runner | +20% | Breedable |
| Nimble | +10% | Breedable |
| Legend | +15% | Innate on legendaries; not breedable |

Swift + Runner + Nimble ≈ **+60%**. Add Legend ≈ **+75%**.

Also: **Eternal Engine** (+75% max stamina on rideables) helps thin-stam flyers like Jetragon.

## Notes

- Catch the Pal first → gear appears in Technology → spend points → craft at **Pal Gear Workbench**.
- Condensation can add mount speed on some Partner Skills (e.g. Direhowl, Fenglope) up to ~20% at max rank — separate from passives.
- Prefer **Xenolord** over Jetragon for long flights if you hate stamina management; prefer **Jetragon** for raw top speed bursts.
- Ground mounts matter in caves / no-fly fights; Necromus / Paladius / Hartalis are the endgame set.

*Sources: [paldb.cc/Mounts](https://paldb.cc/Mounts), PalMods spawn pages, Game8 Alpha levels. Wild mins can shift by patch/region.*
