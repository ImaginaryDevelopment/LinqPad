# Early-Game Skill Fruits

Quick reference for which Skill Fruits to chase early, where to farm them, and how to use them efficiently.

---

## Top Early Priorities

| Skill | Element | Power | Cooldown | Why take it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Air Blade** | Neutral | 85 | 20s | Best early spam skill. Farming Neutral trees can also roll **Holy Burst** (see watchlist — situational in 1.0). |
| **Rock Burst** | Ground | 130 | 35s | Excellent early / mounted shred if projectiles connect. Later, prefer **Sand Twister** / **Rocky Impact** / **Rock Lance** for Ground. |
| **Lightning Bolt** | Electric | 75 | 16s | Very reliable filler. Stunlocks and bridges gaps while heavier skills are on CD. Still a top Electric fruit into mid-game. |
| **Seed Mine** | Grass | 65 | 13s | Fastest early Grass skill. Lower power, but rapid fire clears packs of small Pals well. |

## Other Skills to Watch For

Keep / buy these when they show up on trees or merchants. Numbers below are roughly **1.0** fruit values (power has been rebalanced since early patches).

### Merchant bargains (buy on sight)

| Skill | Element | ~Power | CD | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Geyser Gush** | Water | 600 | 30s | Often only **1,500g** — cheapest 600-power fruit. Big-hitbox bosses only; whiffs on small targets. |
| **Wind Burst** | Grass | 600 | 30s | Same **1,500g** deal. Vortex around your Pal — pack clear / multi-target. |

### By element (meta fruits)

| Element | Priority fruits | Flex / situational |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Electric** | Lightning Bolt, Thunderstorm, Lightning Streak | **TriSpark** — high uptime, short animation for long fights |
| **Water** | Hydro Laser, Hydro Slicer, Splash | **Geyser Gush** — raid/alpha bodies |
| **Grass** | Circle Vine, Solar Blast, Crosswind | **Wind Burst** — packs; Circle Vine scales with large hitboxes |
| **Ice** | Diamond Rain, Icicle Bullet, Blizzard Spike | **Absolute Frost** (700) — giants only; Blizzard Spike needs both spike + cloud to land |
| **Dark** | Dark Laser, Dark Whisp, Apocalypse | **Psycho Gravity** — strong but breeding-only (not a fruit) |
| **Ground** | Sand Twister, Rocky Impact, Rock Lance | **Rock Burst** — still fine point-blank / aggressive melee |
| **Dragon** | Beam Slicer, Dragon Meteor, Blast Cannon | **Comet Strike** — short CD + splash for packs |
| **Neutral** | Pal Blast, Air Blade, Power Bomb | **Holy Burst** (700) — nerfed; only competitive on huge hitboxes |
| **Fire** | Flame Wall, Fire Ball, Flame Funnel | Weaker fruit pool overall; pair with Grass **Ivy** setups when available so the next Fire hit is buffed |

### Notable singles

- **Holy Burst** (Neutral, 700 / 30s) — still a trophy fruit, but in 1.0 it needs a **large** hitbox. Against normal Pals, Circle Vine / Sand Twister often out-DPS it.
- **Circle Vine** (Grass) — roots across the whole hitbox; one of the best boss skills when the target is big.
- **Sand Twister** (Ground) — strong single-target core; bonus if outer tornadoes connect.
- **Lightning Streak** (Electric) — very short CD filler (~8s); keeps pressure between nukes.
- **Beam Slicer** (Dragon) — best Dragon uptime; clean cast for Dark matchups (e.g. Vanwyrm).
- **Dark Laser** (Dark) — efficiency king of Dark fruits; rare on natural learnsets.
- **Blizzard Spike** (Ice) — classic high-value fruit; freeze + wide coverage + beats Dragons (Jetragon, etc.).

### Fire tip

Fire fruits are the weakest pool on raw reliability (multi-hit skills that miss parts of their damage). Prefer **Flame Wall** for honesty; save Fire Ball / Flame Funnel for when you can land every tick.

## Where to Farm (Low-Level Zones)

Skill Fruit trees are tiered by region. Early game: focus **Tier 1** and **Tier 2**.

### Tier 1

| Zone | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Starting Area / Verdant Brook** | Trees near Fisherman's Point, or cliffs south of Chillet. Basic Fire / Water / Neutral fruits. |
| **Desiccated Desert** | Higher concentration of Ground, Fire, and Electric. Especially good for **Rock Burst**. |
| **Mount Obsidian** | Same Tier 1 mix — Ground / Fire / Electric. |

### Tier 2

| Zone | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Windswept Hills** | Reliable for Tier 2 AoE like **Grass Tornado** and **Sand Tornado** (crowd control). |

## Farming Strategy

Pals can only equip **3 active skills**, so be picky.

### Plant Neutral for Holy Burst

Once you unlock a **Skill Fruit Plantation**, plant **Air Blade** fruits:

- Tree guarantees **two Air Blades**
- ~**3.75%** chance of a third, random fruit — most consistent way to hunt **Holy Burst**
- In 1.0, Holy Burst is a **giant-hitbox** skill, not default BiS — still worth farming for bosses/alphas

### Reroll freely

Teaching a new fruit overwrites an old skill instantly. Don’t hoard weak fruits — test animations/cooldowns on training dummies or weak Pals, then overwrite if they don’t fit.

### STAB (Same Type Attack Bonus)

Match fruit element to Pal type (e.g. Rock Burst on Anubis) for **+20%** damage.

### Check merchants

Wandering / settlement merchants rotate Tier 2 (~3,000g) and Tier 3 (~5,000g) fruits. Always buy **Geyser Gush** and **Wind Burst** if priced at **1,500g**.

## Quick Loadout Ideas

| Role | Suggested fruits |
| --- | --- |
| General early DPS | Air Blade + Lightning Bolt + Rock Burst |
| Pack clear | Seed Mine / Wind Burst / Grass Tornado + Air Blade |
| Mounted combat | Rock Burst + Lightning Bolt |
| Boss / large hitbox | Circle Vine or Geyser Gush + element nuke (Thunderstorm / Dragon Meteor / Absolute Frost) |
| Dark matchups (Vanwyrm, etc.) | Beam Slicer / Dragon Meteor + Water coverage |
| Long-term Neutral farm | Keep planting Air Blade → chase Holy Burst (boss use) |
