# Player Damage Conversion

Pals that change **your** attack element (guns / melee), plus an Attack buff. Two styles:

- **Mounted** — conversion while you ride (need saddle)
- **Activated** — press Partner Skill (no mount required)

Atk % scales with Partner Skill rank (condensation).

**Wild min** = lowest documented wild / Alpha level. Region spawns go higher. `—` = unknown / breed / raid-gated.

See also: [Mounts.md](./Mounts.md) · [Breeding-Sheet.md](./Breeding-Sheet.md)

---

## Gobfin note (classic gun build)

Conversion only changes **element**. It does **not** replace party Attack buffs.

Standard setup:

1. Ride an **infusion mount** (or pop an activate converter).
2. Fill the other four party slots with **Gobfin** (or Gobfin Ignis).
3. Gobfin’s Partner Skill stacks player Attack — every shot is now converted **and** multiplied.

Gobfin wild min ~**14**. Ignis is the Fire variant (same idea).
